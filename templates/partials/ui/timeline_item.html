{% load i18n ptbr %}{% comment %} One entry in a client or cycle timeline. The caller owns the list element, an ordered list carrying the timeline class, because order is the meaning. Context: when -- aware datetime; rendered in BRT by `data_hora`, with a machine-readable `datetime` attribute. summary -- what happened, in pt-BR. actor -- optional: who or what did it ("Ana", "Lembrete automático"). state -- optional evidence state, drawn by `status_badge.html`. state_label -- optional pt-BR word for that state. href -- optional link to the evidence behind the entry. href_label -- its pt-BR label; required when `href` is given. ignored -- optional: true for a recorded observation that did not change business state (AXR1-044). It keeps its time and its place in the order, is drawn quieter, and carries a data-observation attribute whose value is the word ignored, for machines. detail -- optional pt-BR sentence under the summary, e.g. why an ignored observation had no effect. The rail and the dot are neutral on purpose: the state of an entry is carried by its badge, which has a glyph and a word, never by the colour of a dot. {% endcomment %}
