A title is passed alongside the reason, and it is load-bearing: the partial renders nothing at all when it is handed neither, and `_refuse` fills the reason from `str(error)` (`apps/accounts/views.py:61-68`). An error whose string is empty would otherwise take the whole alert with it and leave a page that says only that something is unavailable, in the heading, with no announcement. No link to the team page here, unlike the sibling confirmation. This template is the failure surface of two different flows: an invitee redeeming a link on the platform host, who holds no membership and would be refused by that page, and a firm user whose issue form was rejected, who came from it. A destination correct for one of them is a dead end for the other, so the guidance names the person to ask rather than a URL neither may share. Which is exactly why the guidance heading carries the same glyph the confirmation puts on its link to the team page. There the mark leads to a destination; here it stands in for one, because the way forward is a person on that team and there is no address this page may send either reader to. Marking the section as "the team" is the closest thing to wayfinding available, and it is deliberately NOT on the refusal above it: the alert already carries the error ramp, the interrupting role and a sentence, and a warning glyph there would add a fourth copy of one fact. {% endcomment %}
