No number is printed. `docs/lgpd.md` documents exactly one figure — two business days, in the incident runbook of section 4 — and that clock belongs to notifying the ANPD after a breach, not to answering a data subject. Reusing it here would read as a promise, be wrong, and be wrong in the direction the person reading it would act on. The deadline is therefore named by its source, art. 19, which is the same citation `apps/audit/models.py:261` gives for why this request carries a reply address at all. When the encarregado's own service window is written down, this is the line that gets the figure. No protocol number either. The view passes none, the identifier lives on the request row and on the platform event raised beside it, and inventing a reference the support channel would not recognise is worse than admitting the page has none. The two paragraphs after the deadline are not filler: they are the routing rule of section 1 and the retention carve-out of section 5, which are the two things a requester is most likely to be surprised by later. Someone told now that fiscal documents survive an erasure request reads the eventual answer as the law working; someone told for the first time in that answer reads it as a refusal. {% endcomment %}
